🧵 Kalaasetu — From Craft to Market

«Your Craft. Your Voice. Your Market. 🇮🇳»

Kalaasetu is an AI-powered digital marketplace designed to connect India's artisans directly with buyers, while making digital commerce accessible even to artisans with limited digital literacy.

We combine AI, voice interaction, multilingual support, and a simple visual interface to help artisans showcase their crafts, reach new markets, and grow their livelihoods.

✨ Why Kalaasetu?

Millions of Indian artisans have incredible skills but face a major barrier: digital access.

Traditional online marketplaces often expect users to type, navigate complicated menus, understand English, and manage digital listings themselves.

Kalaasetu changes that.

🎤 Voice First

Artisans can interact with the platform using their voice instead of typing.

🌐 Multilingual

Designed to support Indian languages such as Tamil, Hindi, Telugu, and English.

🤖 AI Assistance

AI helps artisans with:

- Product descriptions
- Product categorization
- Price suggestions
- Market discovery
- Buyer matching

🛍️ Direct Marketplace

Buyers can discover unique handmade products and learn the story behind the artisan and their craft.

📊 Impact Dashboard

Government and organizations can view insights such as artisan participation, product categories, market reach, and economic impact.

🔄 How It Works

Artisan

"Login → Choose Language → Voice Onboarding → Add Craft → AI-Assisted Listing → Publish → Orders → Earnings"

Buyer

"Discover → Explore Crafts → Meet the Artisan → Buy → Track Order"
